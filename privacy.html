Privacy Policy

Dairy Round

Dairy Round keeps your records on your phone. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server. Nothing you enter is sent to us, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

What the app stores

Dairy Round records farm visit information you enter yourself: clients and their farms, paddocks and pasture covers, herds and body condition scores, feed reserves and supplement allocations, and any notes you write. All of it is held in a database on your device.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you choose to send it. The app makes no network connections of its own. It has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party services.

Two features move information off the phone, and both are actions you take deliberately:

  • Backups. You create a backup file and choose where it goes — iCloud Drive, email, or another destination on your device. We never receive it.
  • Visit reports. You can produce a PDF of a visit and share it. Where it goes is your choice.

Once you have shared a file, it is subject to the privacy terms of wherever you sent it.

Who can see your data

Only you, and anyone with access to your device or to files you have shared. The developer cannot see your records, and could not retrieve them if asked.

Keeping it safe

Your data is protected by your device's own passcode and encryption. Because the app holds the only copy, we recommend taking regular backups and keeping one somewhere other than the phone. If you lose the device without a backup, the records cannot be recovered by us or by anyone else.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its database and every record in it. Backup files you have already saved or sent elsewhere are not affected, and must be deleted separately if you want them gone.

Children

Dairy Round is a tool for farm consultants and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date.
