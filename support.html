Dairy Round

What it is

Dairy Round records dairy farm consultancy visits: pasture covers paddock by paddock, body condition scoring, herd figures and grazing rotation, supplements, and feed on hand. It works entirely on the phone, so it keeps working in a paddock with no signal.

New to the app?

How to use it walks through setting up a farm and recording your first visit, and explains what the screens are telling you.

Backing up

Your records live on this phone and nowhere else, so a backup is the only way to get them onto another one.

  1. Open the app and tap the backup button at the bottom of the clients list.
  2. Tap Back up now. The files are saved on the phone, under Files → On My iPhone → Dairy Round → Backups.
  3. Tap Send backup and save a copy somewhere off the phone — iCloud Drive, or email it to yourself.

Each backup produces two files: a .json that can restore everything, and a .csv you can open in a spreadsheet.

Restoring onto a new phone

  1. Install Dairy Round and open it.
  2. Tap the backup button, then Restore from a file.
  3. Pick your backup file. You will be shown what it contains before anything changes.
If you lose the phone without a backup, the records cannot be recovered. There is no copy on a server, by design — which is why the app reminds you when a backup is overdue.

Common questions

Why is there no sign-in? There is no server to sign in to. Your data never leaves the device unless you send it, so there is no account to protect. Your phone's passcode is what keeps the records private.

Do I need a connection? No. Everything works offline, including entering visits, producing reports, and taking backups.

Covers are typed in hundreds. Type 25 for 2500 KgDM/ha. The app queries anything that looks like a typo before it affects your averages.
